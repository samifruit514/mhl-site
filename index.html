Manual Hockey League

An online multiplayer ice hockey game by archambo.gg

Grab your stick and play real-time online hockey against other players. You control the stick yourself: aim your passes, time your shots and check your opponents. Play as a skater or in goal, team up with friends, and earn coins to unlock sticks, gear and cosmetics.

Platforms

  • Android: Google Play (early access in Canada)
  • iPhone and iPad: App Store (coming soon)
  • Meta Quest 3 and 3S: Meta Horizon Store (coming soon), played in VR with Touch controllers
