Privacy Policy

Manual Hockey League · Effective date: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what information the Manual Hockey League mobile game (the "Game") collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can have it deleted. The Game is developed and operated by archambo.gg ("we", "us").

1. Information we collect

WhatWhy
Device identifier: an ID from your device, or a random ID generated by the Game To create your game account automatically and sign you back in. You do not enter a name, email or password.
Username: generated for you, or one you choose Shown to other players in matches, lobbies and friend lists.
Social data: your friends list, party/lobby membership, and whether you are online For the friends, invite and party features. Your friends can see when you are online.
Game data: match results, trophies, in-game coins, and cosmetic items you own or equip To run matchmaking, keep your progress and show your items.
Technical data: IP address, connection information, app version, and server logs To connect you to game servers, keep the Game working and secure, and investigate problems.
Gameplay analytics: events such as matchmaking started, match found, match result and duration, plus basic device information To understand how the Game is played and improve it (via Unity Analytics).
Advertising data: advertising ID, device information, IP address and ad interactions To show optional rewarded video ads (for example, to double your match coins) and measure them. Collected by our ad partners, listed below.

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, or payment information. The Game has no real-money purchases; in-game coins can only be earned by playing.

2. Third-party services

We use these service providers, which process data on our behalf or under their own policies:

  • Nakama (game backend, run by us on our own servers) and Amazon Web Services (hosting) store your account, social and game data.
  • Unity Analytics: gameplay analytics. Unity Privacy Policy
  • Unity LevelPlay (ad mediation) and the ad networks it uses:
    • Unity Ads: Privacy Policy
    • Google AdMob: Google Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the providers above, or if required by law.

3. Advertising choices

Ads in the Game are optional: they only play when you choose to watch one. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads).

4. How long we keep data

We keep your account, social and game data while your account exists. Server logs and analytics data are kept for a limited time and then deleted or aggregated. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your account data within 30 days.

5. Deleting your account and data

To delete your Manual Hockey League account and its data, email SUPPORT_EMAIL with the subject "Delete my account" and include your in-game username.

We will delete your account, username, friends list, party data, match history, trophies, coins and items within 30 days, and ask Unity Analytics to delete your analytics data. Data held by ad networks is governed by their policies (see section 2). You can also ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at the same address.

Uninstalling the Game does not delete your account on our servers.

6. Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the Game, contact us and we will delete the account.

7. Security and international transfers

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on servers we control or our providers operate. Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including Canada and the United States.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game or its store listing.
